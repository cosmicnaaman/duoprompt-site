Support

Get help

Email duoprompt.help@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

You can also open a public support request on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Don't include anything private there.

Common questions

The camera preview is black or says the camera is unavailable.

Check that camera access is on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > DuoPrompt, and close other apps that might be using the camera. You can still write and rehearse scripts without the camera.

My take says "Interrupted".

Recording stops if the phone locks, a call comes in, or you leave the app. DuoPrompt keeps everything recorded up to that point and marks the take "Interrupted" so you know it ended early.

"Save to Photos" asks for permission.

DuoPrompt only asks to add videos to your library. It never reads your existing photos.

Where are my scripts and takes stored?

Only on your iPhone. DuoPrompt has no account and no cloud storage. Use Share or Save to Photos to keep a copy elsewhere.

What does DuoPrompt Pro include?

The free version includes up to 3 saved scripts and takes up to 3 minutes long. Pro removes both limits. Your existing takes stay playable and shareable whether or not you subscribe.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

To restore, open the Pro screen in DuoPrompt and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple Account. To cancel, go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap "Manage subscription" on the Pro screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
