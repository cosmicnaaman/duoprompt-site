Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

DuoPrompt is a teleprompter app for recording video on iPhone. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

The short version

  • DuoPrompt does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We don't run any servers for the app.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your scripts and video takes stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share or export them.

Information stored on your device

The scripts you write or import, the video takes you record, and your app settings are saved in DuoPrompt's private storage on your iPhone. We cannot see or access them. They may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups, depending on your own device settings. Deleting a script or take in the app, or deleting the app, removes them from the app's storage.

Camera and microphone

DuoPrompt asks for camera and microphone access so it can show a live preview and record your video with sound. Recordings are saved only on your device. You can turn off access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security. Without it you can still write and rehearse scripts.

Speech recognition (Follow my voice)

If you turn on Follow my voice, DuoPrompt asks for speech recognition and microphone access so the script can move as you read aloud. Your speech is recognized on your iPhone only, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. DuoPrompt never sends, stores or keeps the audio or the recognized words; they are used only to move the script and are discarded right away. If you leave Follow my voice off, speech recognition is never used.

Photos

If you tap "Save to Photos", DuoPrompt asks for permission to add videos to your photo library. It asks for add-only access and never reads, browses or uploads your existing photos.

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the take is handed to the app or service you pick (for example Messages or AirDrop). What happens next is covered by that app's or service's own privacy policy.

Purchases

DuoPrompt Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock Pro features. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Children

DuoPrompt does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact
